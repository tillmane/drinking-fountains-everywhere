Help get this resource afloat!

Fountains For All is currently in early pilot mode -- aka working "as-is". You can help build momentum and make it great by rating fountains, reporting missing or shut off fountains, and by providing feedback (click "Share your feedback!" at the bottom of the map).

Access to clean drinking water is a basic human right. Our goal: a fountain within 300 meters, wherever you are.
